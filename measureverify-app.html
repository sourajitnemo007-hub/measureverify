About MeasureVerify

Online Verification & Legal Metrology Management Platform

Purpose of the System

MeasureVerify is designed as a unified digital platform for managing weighing and measuring instrument verification. It brings customers, shopkeepers, Legal Metrology Officers and administrators onto one system.

Main Modules

  • Stakeholder Registration
  • Instrument Registration
  • Online Verification Applications
  • Inspection Scheduling
  • Digital Inspection Records
  • Digital Certificates
  • QR Certificate Verification
  • Validity and Expiry Tracking
  • Customer Complaints
  • Officer and Admin Dashboards
  • Reports and Monitoring
